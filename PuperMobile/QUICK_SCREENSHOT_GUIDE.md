## 📱 How to Get Screenshots (Easiest Method)

Since your app is running on your phone, this is the easiest way:

### Step 1: Take Screenshots on Your iPhone

1. **Open your Püper app** on your iPhone
2. **Navigate to each screen** you want to show:
   - Map view with restrooms
   - Restroom details with ratings
   - Filter/search screen
   - Add restroom screen
   - Any other key features

3. **Take screenshots**:
   - **iPhone X or newer**: Press `Side Button + Volume Up` at the same time
   - **iPhone 8 or older**: Press `Home Button + Power Button` at the same time

4. **Take 3-10 screenshots** showing different features

### Step 2: Transfer to Your Mac

**Option A: AirDrop** (Easiest)
1. On iPhone: Open Photos app
2. Select the screenshots
3. Tap Share → AirDrop → Your Mac
4. Screenshots appear on your Mac Desktop

**Option B: iCloud Photos**
1. Make sure iCloud Photos is enabled
2. Screenshots sync automatically
3. Open Photos app on Mac
4. Find screenshots

**Option C: Email**
1. On iPhone: Select screenshots
2. Share → Mail
3. Email to yourself
4. Download on Mac

### Step 3: Resize Screenshots (If Needed)

Your iPhone screenshots might not be the exact size needed. Here's how to resize:

**Using Preview (Mac):**
1. Open screenshot in Preview
2. **Tools** → **Adjust Size**
3. Set dimensions:
   - **iPhone 6.5"**: 1284 x 2778 pixels
   - **iPad**: 2048 x 2732 pixels
4. Uncheck "Scale proportionally" if needed
5. Click **OK**
6. **File** → **Save**

**Using Online Tool:**
1. Go to: https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image
2. Upload your screenshot
3. Select "Resize by pixels"
4. Enter dimensions:
   - iPhone: 1284 x 2778
   - iPad: 2048 x 2732
5. Click "Resize Image"
6. Download

### Step 4: Create iPad Screenshots

**Option A: Resize iPhone Screenshots**
