# Privacy Policy for Püper

Last updated: October 21, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Püper ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application and web service (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Location Information
- We collect precise location information to help you find nearby restrooms
- Location access is only used when you actively use the restroom-finding features
- You can disable location access through your device settings

### User-Provided Information
- Restroom reviews and ratings
- Photos of restrooms (optional)
- Profile information (if you choose to create an account)

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Usage data (how you interact with the app)
- IP address
- Browser type and version (for web app)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
- Providing and improving the Service
- Finding and ranking nearby restrooms based on your location
- Displaying restroom reviews and ratings
- Communicating with you about the Service
- Analyzing usage patterns to improve user experience
- Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:
- Service providers who assist us in operating the Service
- Law enforcement or regulatory authorities when required by law
- Other users in aggregated, anonymized form for restroom rankings

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations.
